Most consequential, as the drafts themselves characterize them:

- B.14: universal-kernel population context rank is infinite on Mess3, challenging any intrinsic-population interpretation of the r_ctx ≈ k−1 hypothesis.
- B.45–B.46: the self-stitch counterexample invalidates an unconditional Section 8 scalar predictor/bound without readout/block commutation or its correction.
- B.32 and S.4: the rank-dependent IPR upper bound is 1/r, and minimum IPR does not certify indicator recovery.
- A.7: the retained-site gradient expansion equals the full gradient only when excluded-site and direct-term contributions jointly vanish.
