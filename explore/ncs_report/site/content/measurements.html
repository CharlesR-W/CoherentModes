1. What do the eigenfunctions separate, layer by layer?

$\eta^2$ of an eigenfunction against a grouping of the items is the share of its variance that lies between the groups' means; it needs no sets.

2. What do linked contexts share, layer by layer?

For a grouping, $S$ is the share of a walk's off-diagonal mass on pairs in the same group and $S_0$ its value for a uniform walk; the plotted excess is $(S - S_0)/(1 - S_0)$. Bands are 50 permutations of the grouping, in the same units. $\tilde\kappa$ is the excess Bhattacharyya similarity of the trained model's next-token distributions over linked pairs from different snippets; $\tilde\kappa_{|T}$ is the same against a null that permutes predictions only among contexts with the same token at $t$.
