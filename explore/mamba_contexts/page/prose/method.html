Construction

The construction is inherited from v1 (../mamba_coherent/SPEC.md) and, through it, from the Pythia study; only the items, views, blocks and statistics below are new (SPEC.md is the authority).

  1. Items. For each of the {{fact:n_snippets}} v1 snippets, {{fact:per_snippet}} distinct positions t from {{fact:t_min}} to {{fact:t_max}}, drawn with a per-snippet seed; item (i, t) is the prefix [EOT] + tokens 1..t. No two items share the same multiset of their last 16 tokens (label-free de-duplication; {{fact:dedup_redraws}} redraws). Items are stored in a seeded random order. Lags τ ∈ {{{fact:lags}}}: the same item read τ tokens later.
  2. Views. point: the raw residual after block l. state: layer l's recurrent state, the Gram average of its conv buffer c and SSM state z. whole: the Gram average of all 48 constituents. References (never in a block): bag (mean input embedding of tokens 1..t), recent (of tokens t−15..t), pred ($\sqrt{p_t}$, the trained model's next-token distribution).
  3. Operator per snapshot (inherited): cosine on unit-normalised vectors, union kNN graph with k = {{fact:k}} and floor $10^{-6}$, Sinkhorn balancing, lazy walk $P$.
  4. Blocks. B-point (point, L1..L24, the headline), its thirds, B-state (state, L1..L24), B-lag-whole (whole at every τ), B-lag-point (point L16 at every τ). The block operator is the running mean of its snapshots' operators; its eigenpairs give the eigenfunctions $\psi_k$ and, by SEBA at r ∈ {{{fact:ranks}}}, the coherent sets.
  5. Statistics. Excess walk shares against each grouping with exact chance and a {{fact:n_perm}}-permutation band; κ̃ and κ̃|T (pairs from the same snippet excluded); η² of ψ₁..ψ{{fact:n_eta}} against each grouping; AMI and matched Jaccard of the hard sets; span agreement ar between objects; lag retention; zoom trees; and the multi-way ratio $\hat\Upsilon(k) = \mu_k/\hat\rho(k)$ with its cap $2\mu_k/\mu_{k-1}$.

Reading rule for clusters (SPEC 5). $\hat\Upsilon(k)$ uses two label-free roundings (SEBA argmax and k-means on ψ₁..ψk−1) and keeps the larger; any complete partition gives a lower bound on Peng, Sun and Zanetti's $\Upsilon(k)$, and the higher-order Cheeger inequality caps every partition at $2\mu_k/\mu_{k-1}$. Certified: $\hat\Upsilon(k) \ge \theta$. Excluded: cap below θ. Undetermined otherwise; that is not evidence of absence. θ = {{fact:theta}} was fixed on the fixture before the run ({{fact:theta_rule}}) and is a reading convention, not a recovery guarantee.

Hazards

  • K1 has no null. It compares two AMIs; a structureless walk can "meet" it by noise. Read it with K1b and with F4's η² against its band.
  • Token identity explains much. Token classes are functions of the token at t, and the token predicts much of pt, so high C̃c or κ̃ alone means "grouped by current token". Only κ̃|T above its band says more. Items whose token is unique in the probe keep their own prediction in the null (B-point share: {{fact:kappa.trained.Bpoint.unique_share}}).
  • κ is normalised by the walk mass it scores (README deviation 2); the SPEC form is stored as kappa_raw. Block κ is κ of the block's $\bar P$, not a mean of its snapshots' κ.
  • θ is a convention. "Excluded" is a theorem about the operator; "certified" depends on θ; "undetermined" is silence.
  • Layouts are display only (UMAP of ψ₁..ψ₁₆, fixed seed). Distances in the picture are not the method's.
  • Sets are hard SEBA sets on assigned items; unassigned items are grey and excluded from AMI.
  • Source is confounded (code and forum mix; Overview, point 4), so source-based targets (K2) are read with that in mind.
