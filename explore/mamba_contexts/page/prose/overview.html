The question. The first Mamba study ("v1", explore/mamba_coherent/) found little in mamba-130m: its headline sets recovered the five document sources, which a bag of tokens also recovers, and almost none of its zoom cuts read as a clear split. Were we looking at the wrong things?

The answer: partly

v1 missed token-level organisation, because it scored its sets mainly against document source. The follow-up (v2) makes that organisation visible. It does not find clear clusters, and it finds no source grouping stronger than a bag-of-tokens reference gives. Three of the seven pre-registered targets failed.

  • Two separate problems in v1.
    • It scored the wrong labels. Its "depth blocks" (one position, read across layers) group contexts by the kind of token just read (newlines, function words, punctuation, LaTeX), but v1 scored them only against source.
    • There were no clear clusters to recover. Its spectra have no strong gap, and changing the labels would not have created one.
  • v2's items are single token contexts: {{fact:n_items}} prefixes, each ending at a sampled token. Their coherent sets are scored against the token, the next token, the snippet and the model's own predictions.
  • The residual stream groups contexts by the class of the token just read. Agreement with that class is {{fact:ami8.trained.Bpoint.Cc}} on a 0 (chance) to 1 scale, against {{fact:ami8.trained.Bpoint.W}} with source. On top of that there is a small association with what the model predicts next, beyond what sharing the token explains. These groupings fade within one or two tokens of further reading.
  • The recurrent state groups contexts by source (agreement {{fact:ami8.trained.whole.W}}), and its groupings mostly last at least sixteen tokens.
    • A plain bag of the prefix's tokens groups by source just as strongly, so this probe cannot show that the state tracks the document beyond its vocabulary.
    • The state also carries the token-class and predictive associations, which the bag does not.
  • Not found.
    • No object is certified as clustered at the tested numbers of parts. The residual block is excluded under the θ = {{fact:theta}} rule; the state blocks leave some cases undetermined (not evidence either way).
    • The whole state keeps more of its walk within a snippet than the residual block does (excess share {{fact:tgt.K2.U_whole}} against {{fact:tgt.K2.U_Bpoint}}), but the gap is below the 0.2 the target required (K2).
    • Predictive coherence does not rise by the predicted amount from layer 4 to layer 24 (K3).

Targets met: {{fact:v.met_list}}; not met: {{fact:v.not_met_list}}; every control passes. Read on: Findings (claims, evidence, alternatives), Explorer (the sets themselves), Measurements (one figure per question).

The diagnosis of v1 in detail

v1 had two kinds of block, and the diagnosis concerns both. A reading block follows one layer along a snippet (positions 4..128) and averages the walks; a depth block takes one position and averages across layers L4..L24. AMI below is the agreement between a block's hard sets and a labelling (0 = chance, 1 = identical); r is the number of sets.

  1. Reading blocks were scored against source, and recover it the way a bag of tokens does. Averaging along a snippet keeps what persists along a document, which on that probe is its vocabulary. The {{fact:v1.n_reading}} reading blocks' sets agree with source at AMI {{fact:v1.reading.src.range}}, and a bag-of-tokens reference finds the same partition.
  2. Depth blocks group by the kind of token just read, which v1 never scored.
    • Their sets agree with the current token's class at mean AMI {{fact:v1.depth.cc.by_r}} for r = {{fact:v1.r_list}}, against {{fact:v1.depth.src.by_r}} with source.
    • The class signal holds within one source (AMI {{fact:v1.cc_within_source.range}}), while source within one class is weak ({{fact:v1.src_within_class.range}}).
    • {{fact:v1.sets.n_class70}} of the {{fact:v1.sets.n}} depth-block sets at r = 8 are at least 70% one class: function words, line ends, punctuation, LaTeX. The rest are mixtures or broad word buckets.
  3. Separately, there were no clear clusters to recover.
    • v1 called a cut "clear" when its two-way ratio Υ reached 3. {{fact:v1.tree.ups_ge3}} of {{fact:v1.tree.internal}} cuts did, and {{fact:v1.tree.balanced2pct}} fell within 2% of an even split, which is what a cut with no bottleneck does.
    • Asking for more parts does not help. At v1's two block roots, the multi-way ratio $\hat\Upsilon(k)$ for k = {{fact:v1.T1.k.range}} parts is {{fact:v1.T1.ups.range}} and {{fact:v1.T4.ups.range}}, against the same threshold of 3. Even the partition into the five sources scores only {{fact:v1.T1.src5}} and {{fact:v1.T4.src5}}.
    • The spectral cap $2\mu_k/\mu_{k-1}$, which no partition can exceed, is {{fact:v1.T1.cap.range}} and {{fact:v1.T4.cap.range}}. Where it is above 3 (T4 at {{fact:v1.T4.cap_ge3}}; T1 at {{fact:v1.T1.cap_ge3}}) a clear partition is possible in principle, but none of the tested ones reaches it, and the spectra show no large gap at any k.
  4. The walk merges two of the five sources. In v1's whole-state snapshot the walk crosses between code and forum more often than chance (mixing ratio {{fact:v1.X.stack_L24_t128.code_forum}}, where 1 means no avoidance and the null band tops out at {{fact:v1.X.stack_L24_t128.null_hi}}). Every other pair is avoided (at most {{fact:v1.X.stack_L24_t128.others_max}}). This describes how the model treats the texts, not a flaw in the probe's design; it means source is a roughly four-group labelling.

What v2 looks at instead

  • Items. A token context is the prefix of a v1 snippet ending at a sampled token t: {{fact:per_snippet}} positions in each of {{fact:n_snippets}} snippets, t = {{fact:t_min}}..{{fact:t_max}}, N = {{fact:n_items}}.
  • Views at every layer (diagram above):
    • the residual after each block (point);
    • each layer's recurrent state (state);
    • the whole state.
    The headline block, B-point, is the residual across L1..L24; B-state does the same for the states. Reading is measured by re-reading the same items τ = {{fact:lags}} tokens later.
  • Groupings to score against.
    • Label-free: the token at t, the token at t+1, the snippet.
    • The model's own outputs: its top prediction, and predictive coherence, which is how similar the next-token distributions of contexts the walk links are. Its conditional form asks the same beyond what sharing the token explains.
    • For validation only: source and a {{fact:n_classes}}-class token taxonomy.
  • References that use no model state at all: a bag of the prefix's tokens, the last 16 tokens, and the model's next-token distribution. They show how much of a grouping plain token statistics already give.
  • Readouts that need no clusters: walk shares, η² of each eigenfunction, retention along reading, span agreement. There is also a multi-way clustering certificate with three outcomes (certified, excluded, undetermined). The formulas are in Method.

Where each question is answered

  1. What the residual's depth sets group: the class of the token just read, plus a small predictive part beyond the token (Findings 1–2, Measurements F2 and F4).
  2. What the state's sets group: source, matched by the bag reference for source but not for token class or prediction (Finding 3, F2 and F4).
  3. How long sets last along reading: residual sets about one or two tokens; state sets mostly beyond sixteen, with one fast-fading exception (Finding 4, F5).
  4. Clear multi-way structure: not certified anywhere; excluded under the θ rule for the residual, with undetermined cases left for the state (Finding 5, F3 and F6).
