The question. The first Mamba study ("v1", explore/mamba_coherent/) found little in mamba-130m: its headline sets recovered the five document sources, which a bag of tokens also recovers, and almost none of its zoom cuts read as a clear split. Were we looking at the wrong things?

The diagnosis of v1 in detail

v1 had two kinds of block, and the diagnosis concerns both. A reading block follows one layer along a snippet (positions 4..128) and averages the walks; a depth block takes one position and averages across layers L4..L24. AMI below is the agreement between a block's hard sets and a labelling (0 = chance, 1 = identical); r is the number of sets.

  1. Reading blocks were scored against source, and recover it the way a bag of tokens does. Averaging along a snippet keeps what persists along a document, which on that probe is its vocabulary. The 18 reading blocks' sets agree with source at AMI 0.46 to 0.86, and a bag-of-tokens reference finds the same partition.
  2. Depth blocks group by the kind of token just read, which v1 never scored.
    • Their sets agree with the current token's class at mean AMI 0.35/0.41/0.46/0.47/0.47 for r = 2/3/4/6/8, against 0.14/0.12/0.13/0.14/0.15 with source.
    • The class signal holds within one source (AMI 0.32 to 0.48), while source within one class is weak (0.08 to 0.15).
    • 16 of the 48 depth-block sets at r = 8 are at least 70% one class: function words, line ends, punctuation, LaTeX. The rest are mixtures or broad word buckets.
  3. Separately, there were no clear clusters to recover.
    • v1 called a cut "clear" when its two-way ratio Υ reached 3. 1 of 28 cuts did, and 22 fell within 2% of an even split, which is what a cut with no bottleneck does.
    • Asking for more parts does not help. At v1's two block roots, the multi-way ratio $\hat\Upsilon(k)$ for k = 2 to 8 parts is 1.09 to 1.36 and 1.00 to 1.58, against the same threshold of 3. Even the partition into the five sources scores only 1.16 and 1.18.
    • The spectral cap $2\mu_k/\mu_{k-1}$, which no partition can exceed, is 2.03 to 2.85 and 2.08 to 3.58. Where it is above 3 (T4 at k = 2, 4; T1 at no k) a clear partition is possible in principle, but none of the tested ones reaches it, and the spectra show no large gap at any k.
  4. The walk merges two of the five sources. In v1's whole-state snapshot the walk crosses between code and forum more often than chance (mixing ratio 1.36, where 1 means no avoidance and the null band tops out at 1.06). Every other pair is avoided (at most 0.37). This describes how the model treats the texts, not a flaw in the probe's design; it means source is a roughly four-group labelling.

What v2 looks at instead

  • Items. A token context is the prefix of a v1 snippet ending at a sampled token t: 8 positions in each of 150 snippets, t = 16..112, N = 1,200.
  • Views at every layer (diagram above):
    • the residual after each block (point);
    • each layer's recurrent state (state);
    • the whole state.
    The headline block, B-point, is the residual across L1..L24; B-state does the same for the states. Reading is measured by re-reading the same items τ = 0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 tokens later.
  • Groupings to score against.
    • Label-free: the token at t, the token at t+1, the snippet.
    • The model's own outputs: its top prediction, and predictive coherence, which is how similar the next-token distributions of contexts the walk links are. Its conditional form asks the same beyond what sharing the token explains.
    • For validation only: source and a 12-class token taxonomy.
  • References that use no model state at all: a bag of the prefix's tokens, the last 16 tokens, and the model's next-token distribution. They show how much of a grouping plain token statistics already give.
  • Readouts that need no clusters: walk shares, η² of each eigenfunction, retention along reading, span agreement. There is also a multi-way clustering certificate with three outcomes (certified, excluded, undetermined). The formulas are in Method.

Construction

The construction is inherited from v1 (../mamba_coherent/SPEC.md) and, through it, from the Pythia study; only the items, views, blocks and statistics below are new (SPEC.md is the authority).

  1. Items. For each of the 150 v1 snippets, 8 distinct positions t from 16 to 112, drawn with a per-snippet seed; item (i, t) is the prefix [EOT] + tokens 1..t. No two items share the same multiset of their last 16 tokens (label-free de-duplication; 5 redraws). Items are stored in a seeded random order. Lags τ ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16}: the same item read τ tokens later.
  2. Views. point: the raw residual after block l. state: layer l's recurrent state, the Gram average of its conv buffer c and SSM state z. whole: the Gram average of all 48 constituents. References (never in a block): bag (mean input embedding of tokens 1..t), recent (of tokens t−15..t), pred ($\sqrt{p_t}$, the trained model's next-token distribution).
  3. Operator per snapshot (inherited): cosine on unit-normalised vectors, union kNN graph with k = 15 and floor $10^{-6}$, Sinkhorn balancing, lazy walk $P$.
  4. Blocks. B-point (point, L1..L24, the headline), its thirds, B-state (state, L1..L24), B-lag-whole (whole at every τ), B-lag-point (point L16 at every τ). The block operator is the running mean of its snapshots' operators; its eigenpairs give the eigenfunctions $\psi_k$ and, by SEBA at r ∈ {2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32}, the coherent sets.
  5. Statistics. Excess walk shares against each grouping with exact chance and a 50-permutation band; κ̃ and κ̃|T (pairs from the same snippet excluded); η² of ψ₁..ψ16 against each grouping; AMI and matched Jaccard of the hard sets; span agreement ar between objects; lag retention; zoom trees; and the multi-way ratio $\hat\Upsilon(k) = \mu_k/\hat\rho(k)$ with its cap $2\mu_k/\mu_{k-1}$.

Reading rule for clusters (SPEC 5). $\hat\Upsilon(k)$ uses two label-free roundings (SEBA argmax and k-means on ψ₁..ψk−1) and keeps the larger; any complete partition gives a lower bound on Peng, Sun and Zanetti's $\Upsilon(k)$, and the higher-order Cheeger inequality caps every partition at $2\mu_k/\mu_{k-1}$. Certified: $\hat\Upsilon(k) \ge \theta$. Excluded: cap below θ. Undetermined otherwise; that is not evidence of absence. θ = 3 was fixed on the fixture before the run (fixed) and is a reading convention, not a recovery guarantee.

The measured quantities

$$ \tilde S_G = \frac{S_G - S^0_G}{1 - S^0_G},\qquad S_G = \frac1N \sum_{i \ne j,\ g(i) = g(j)} P_{ij},\qquad \tilde\kappa_{|T} = \frac{\kappa - \kappa^{T}}{1 - \kappa^{T}},\qquad \kappa = \frac{\sum_{i,j\ \text{diff. snippet}} P_{ij}\,\mathrm{BC}(p_i, p_j)}{\sum_{i,j\ \text{diff. snippet}} P_{ij}} $$
where $P$ is a snapshot's (or block's) walk on the $N$ items, $g$ a grouping of the items (token at $t$, token at $t+1$, snippet, the model's top-1 prediction, source, token class), $S^0_G$ the exact chance level, $\mathrm{BC}$ the Bhattacharyya coefficient of the trained model's next-token distributions $p_i, p_j$, and $\kappa^T$ the mean of $\kappa$ when the prediction vectors are permuted within groups of items sharing the token at $t$ (50 permutations). $\tilde S = 0$ is chance, $1$ means the walk never leaves the group.

Hazards

  • K1 has no null. It compares two AMIs; a structureless walk can "meet" it by noise. Read it with K1b and with F4's η² against its band.
  • Token identity explains much. Token classes are functions of the token at t, and the token predicts much of pt, so high C̃c or κ̃ alone means "grouped by current token". Only κ̃|T above its band says more. Items whose token is unique in the probe keep their own prediction in the null (B-point share: 0%).
  • κ is normalised by the walk mass it scores (README deviation 2); the SPEC form is stored as kappa_raw. Block κ is κ of the block's $\bar P$, not a mean of its snapshots' κ.
  • θ is a convention. "Excluded" is a theorem about the operator; "certified" depends on θ; "undetermined" is silence.
  • Layouts are display only (UMAP of ψ₁..ψ₁₆, fixed seed). Distances in the picture are not the method's.
  • Sets are hard SEBA sets on assigned items; unassigned items are grey and excluded from AMI.
  • Source is confounded (code and forum mix; Overview, point 4), so source-based targets (K2) are read with that in mind.

Deviations from SPEC.md (quoted from README.md)

  1. De-duplication compares the multiset of the last 16 tokens, not the sequence. recent (mean embedding of tokens t-15..t) is a function of that multiset, so two items whose windows are permutations of each other (on the real probe, typically (i, t) and (i, t+1) when the token leaving the window equals the one entering) are exact duplicates in a reference cell and the v1 duplicate assertion stops U2. The fixture hit this. A shared sequence is a shared multiset, so this is the stricter reading of "share their last 16 tokens". On the real probe: 22 redraws; no two items share their bag multiset either.
  2. kappa is normalised by the walk mass it scores: kappa = sum_{different snippet} P_ij BC_ij / sum_{different snippet} P_ij, which equals SPEC's (1/N) sum when no same-snippet pair carries walk mass; without it, a walk that stays inside snippets would read as predictively incoherent. The SPEC form is stored as kappa_raw with the mass (mass_diff). The within-token null permutes prediction rows within token groups and keeps the exclusion on the walk pair (i, j), so kappa^T uses the same pairs and normaliser as kappa.
  3. Block statistics are computed on the block's Pbar. Shares on Pbar equal the mean of the cells' (linear in P); kappa is a ratio, so a block's kappa is kappa(Pbar) (K1b reads this), not the mean of its cells' kappa.
  4. Pilot = U1 and U2 on trained, then the gate (SPEC: "U1 and the gate"): the gate's Sinkhorn, duplicate and PN checks are U2 outputs. C1a/C1b run inside U1 trained with v1's code on the 1,000 snippets.
  5. Units added: calib_<seed> (the fixture calibration on Modal CPU; locally it would take about 45 min), diag (a CPU probe of SEBA's cost, below). No U0: U1 copies the probe and asserts its sha256 (v1's U0 established the tokenizer facts on the same file). U1 mounts v1's volume to read act_trained.npy for C3.
  6. The guard reserves per stage, and each (unit, checkpoint) is its own Modal function with its own timeout. v1 reserved every remaining unit at every launch. Here that would be $10.3 at 2x timeouts although the projected study total is $6.1; per stage, the pilot reserves $7.23 and the full run (if the pilot costs as projected) $7.65. The ceiling is still enforced before every dispatch, so nothing can spend past $8; the risk is only that the full run is refused after an expensive pilot.
  7. Groupings and objects: lag cells are scored against T, Cc, U, W (lag 0) and T_tau, without kappa; B-lag-point and B-lag-whole against T, Cc, U, W. recent also gets a spectral object (SPEC lists bag and pred); the references are not computed at init. Set descriptors of lag cells use the lag-0 tokens. The matched-Jaccard null keeps v1's 200-permutation bank; shares, eta^2 and kappa use 50.
  8. Also stored (v1 inheritance): each block's retention of psi_1..8 and of its r = 8 SEBA vectors in its own cells (M-form, v1 block_record), next to the SPEC's lag retention (P-form, chance -1/(N-1)).
  9. eta^2's lambda-weighted mean uses lambda_k(Mbar) (k = 1..16). SEBA at r uses the constant and psi_1..psi_{r-1} (v1 and Froyland's convention), so r = 32 uses the 33rd eigenpair's predecessors only.
  10. Calibration seeding: each block draws from default_rng([seed, k, sizes_index, round(100 s)]) (structureless: [seed, 0, 0, 0]); one structureless block per seed. The SPEC fixes seeds but not how cases derive from them.
  11. Implementation speed-ups that do not change the mathematics: SEBA and k-means for the 9 ranks run concurrently, each single-threaded; UMAP layouts (fixed random_state, single-threaded) run in parallel processes; NUMBA_NUM_THREADS=1 in the image.
  12. The decode was not benchmarked (Stage A spends nothing on the model). The projection takes v1's measured decode (42 s per pass over the 1,000 snippets on an L40S) with a 1.5x margin.
  13. v1's two-way Upsilon in the trees is read against v1's threshold 3.0 (its P1 value); theta for Upsilon_hat(k) is this study's calibration (also 3.0).
