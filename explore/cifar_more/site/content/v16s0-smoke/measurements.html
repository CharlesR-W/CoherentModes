Stability horizons

Horizon $h$ of a block: the largest $k$ such that $\psi_1..\psi_k$ built from two overlapping random {{fact:stab_subset}}-image subsets agree on their {{fact:stab_core}} shared images with every principal angle's squared cosine at least 0.5 (definition). Computed up to $k$ = {{fact:n_vecs}} + 16; values above {{fact:n_vecs}} mean "reproducible beyond the stored modes".

Linear decodability at the final checkpoint

A validation fit, not part of the construction: closed-form ridge regression on one-hot labels and a cosine 20-nearest-neighbour vote, fitted on 10,000 training images (1,000 per class) and scored on all 10,000 test images, at the final checkpoint only.
