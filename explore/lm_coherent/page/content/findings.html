1. Averaging along reading reproduces the sources; other blocks only lean towards them

Claim (observed). At step {{fact:final_step}}, the reading block of each layer from L4 to L12 (positions 4 to 128) gives four coherent sets that match four of the five sources at {{fact:rd.sj.L4_12}} (matched Jaccard: the mean overlap of each set with its best-matching source; null at most {{fact:rd.null}}). Set sizes cap that score (at {{fact:rd.ceiling}} here), and the reading blocks reach {{fact:rd.ratio.L4_12}} of their cap. For L8 the sets are mainly code, poetry, Wikipedia and maths; forum posts are spread over the code set, the maths set and the unassigned snippets. The other blocks are associated with source above chance but do not reproduce the partition: every point cell ({{fact:cell.above_null.point}}), depth block ({{fact:depth.above_null}}) and training block ({{fact:train.above_null}}) beats its own permutation null (each tested separately, not as a family), but single cells reach {{fact:cell.sj.point.range}} (best {{fact:cell.best.point}}), only {{fact:cell.ratio.point.range}} of their caps ({{fact:cell.ceiling.point.range}}), depth blocks {{fact:depth.sj.range}} and training blocks at most {{fact:train.sj.max}}.

The four sets' SEBA vectors (soft, non-negative versions of the sets) have a retention of {{fact:t1.ret_min}} to {{fact:t1.ret_max}} in each of the six cells. That is a smoothness measure under the lazy walk, not the chance of staying in a set: 0.5 of it is added automatically by the walk's laziness (without it, {{fact:t1.ret_nonlazy}}), and the same vectors shuffled over the snippets would score {{fact:t1.ret_base}} on average. So in every cell the vectors are smoother than chance, by a moderate margin.

How I read it (reading). In one cell, a snippet's nearest neighbours are partly the snippets that read the same or a similar token at that position, and those change from one position to the next. Neighbours that persist across positions are snippets of similar text, which in this probe largely means the same source. Averaging the walks keeps the persistent links and dilutes the rest; the averaged walk above is visibly more blocked than any of its cells. Two things make me prefer this reading to "any average of six cells helps": depth blocks also average six cells and reproduce the sources far less, and at one position the token just read stays the same across layers, so the links it makes persist along depth but not along reading. The slab at $t \ge 64$, whose geometry is itself an average over positions, finds the sources about as well ({{fact:cell.sj.slab.t64_128.L2_10}} at L2 to L10).

What else it could be. The four sets need not be the only structure the reading block carries; they are what SEBA reads off its first three eigenfunctions, and the block's spectrum has no clear gap ($\lambda_1 \ldots \lambda_4$ = {{fact:t1.lambda1}}, {{fact:t1.lambda2}}, {{fact:t1.lambda3}}, {{fact:t1.lambda4}}).

2. The sources are close to what token counts give

Claim (observed). The bag reference, the mean input embedding of tokens 1 to $t$, keeps more of its walk within sources than any layer's residual stream at every position ($\tilde W$ {{fact:Wex.bag.t4}} at $t = 4$ and {{fact:Wex.bag.t128}} at $t = 128$, against at most {{fact:Wex.max.point}} for any layer). The L8 reading block's four sets match the bag cell's four sets at $t = 128$ at {{fact:t1_vs_bag.t128}} and the L8 slab cell's at {{fact:t1_vs_slab.L8.t128}} (both label-free comparisons; their own L8 cell at $t = 128$ gives {{fact:t1_vs_point.L8.t128}}).

How I read it (reading). In this probe, a source is mostly a vocabulary: code, maths, poetry and encyclopaedic prose use different tokens. Recovering the sources therefore shows that the construction finds the probe's main grouping, but not that the model adds anything to it; the reading block arrives at nearly the partition that counting tokens gives. A test of structure beyond token statistics needs a grouping that token counts do not reveal, which this probe does not have (checks).

3. Early layers follow the token just read; source builds up over about 32 tokens

Claim (observed; E1 {{fact:v.E1.status}}, H1 {{fact:v.H1.status}}). At step {{fact:final_step}}, the walk's excess share among snippets reading the same token, $\tilde T$, is {{fact:Tex.L2.range}} at L2 at every position and {{fact:Tex.L12.range}} at L12 (E1, an established result about contextual representations, holds). At L2 it is already {{fact:Tex.L2.step0.range}} at step 0. The source share $\tilde W$ is {{fact:Wex.step0.range}} in every cell at step 0. At the final step, in L6 to L12, it rises with position up to $t$ = 32 or 64 and then flattens or falls back (L8: {{fact:Wex.L8.t4}}, {{fact:Wex.L8.t32}}, {{fact:Wex.L8.t128}} at $t$ = 4, 32, 128); at L2 and L4 it creeps up to $t = 128$. Its total gain from $t = 4$ to 128 at L6 to L12 is {{fact:H1.diffs}}, below the 0.2 I predicted, so H1 is not met.

How I read it (reading). The residual at one position carries the current token strongly in early layers, even before training, which is what a residual stream that starts from the token embedding should do. The source signal it adds saturates early: after a few dozen tokens, one more position does not make the snippet's neighbours more source-like. Finding 7 shows that the whole prefix does keep gaining.

4. Maths and poetry part from about the eighth token, but so do other pairs

Claim (observed; H2 {{fact:v.H2.status}}). The pair mixing ratio $X(\text{maths}, \text{poetry})$ first falls below 0.5 at $t = 8$ in L6 to L12 and is {{fact:X.mp.L6_12.t32_128}} for $t \ge 32$. At (L8, 16) it is {{fact:X.mp.L8.t16}} against a permutation band of {{fact:X.mp.L8.t16.band}}: the walk crosses between them about a third as often as chance. At that cell it is the {{fact:X.mp.L8.t16.rank}} separated of the {{fact:X.pairs.n}} source pairs; code and poetry are at {{fact:X.cp.L8.t16}}, wiki and forum at {{fact:X.wf.L8.t16}}, and code and forum are not separated at all ({{fact:X.cf.L8.t16}}). L2 keeps mixing maths and poetry ({{fact:X.mp.L2.range}}). Followed one at a time, the default maths and poetry snippets send most of their walk to their own source in the late layers ({{fact:follow.maths.own.L12.t128}} and {{fact:follow.poetry.own.L12.t128}} at (L12, 128)), yet they share a single-cell $r = 4$ set in {{fact:follow.same_cells}} cells.

How I read it (reading). Maths and poetry are a clear contrast for the walk, but a matter of degree: once a handful of tokens have been read, the walk keeps most pairs of sources apart to some extent, and the pairs differ in how much (code and forum posts, which share programming text, not at all). A single cell's own sets are not organised by source (finding 1), which is why two snippets whose walks go mostly to different sources can still land in the same cell-level set.

5. The zoom cuts follow sources, and none is a clear split

Claim (observed; H5 {{fact:v.H5.status}}). T1's first cut puts {{fact:T1.root.child0}} on the same side, so code and poetry are on opposite sides (H5). Every one of the {{fact:ups.real.n}} splits in T1 to T4 has a clustering ratio between {{fact:ups.real.range}}. On the fixtures, planted splits gave at least {{fact:ups.planted.min}} and structureless splits {{fact:ups.structureless.range}}. No node's kNN graph falls apart. The cut is far from a random split of the walk: its retention in T1's cells is {{fact:T1.root.ret}}, against about 0.5 for a random split. These trees are not the whole picture: single cells at early positions do contain small, nearly isolated groups (finding 9; at $t = 4$ the smallest set has {{fact:loc.minset.t4}} snippets and the walk almost never leaves it), but the zoom's root cannot cut off fewer than {{fact:T1.root.m_min}} snippets, and the trees are built on blocks and on the cell at $t = 128$.

What the comparison licenses, and what it does not. The threshold of {{fact:threshold}} was fixed on synthetic fixtures: isotropic Gaussian noise in 32 dimensions, with or without a planted hierarchy, {{fact:n_items}} items, the same kernel. On those, planted and structureless splits are separated by a factor of four. That licenses one statement: no split here is separated from the rest of the walk by anything like the margin of a planted split. It does not license "the walk is as structureless as noise". The value of $\Upsilon$ for structureless data depends on the dimension, the shape of the covariance and the kernel. A residual stream is 768-dimensional and far from isotropic (finding 9), and no null with that geometry was run.

How I read it (reading). The source structure in the averaged walk looks graded rather than clustered: sources occupy different regions, with wide borders, rather than separate islands with few links between them. The first eigenfunction of T1's block falls smoothly across the snippets rather than in steps (step 4 of the procedure). The check that would test this reading: run the same zoom on surrogate cells with each real cell's covariance and no groups (Gaussian draws matched to the centred covariance), and compare their $\Upsilon$ with the real ones. If the real values sit inside the surrogate range, the walk has no more cluster structure than its covariance implies.

6. The source association appears in the first 512 steps

Claim (observed; H4 {{fact:v.H4.status}}). At (L8, 128), $\tilde W$ is {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step0}} at step 0, {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step512}} at step 512, and {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step2000}}, {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step16000}} and {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step143000}} after that. The L8 reading block's match to sources goes {{fact:rd.sj.L8.by_step}} over the same five checkpoints. Single-cell sets keep moving: against the final checkpoint's sets they score {{fact:tf.t16}} at (L8, 16) for steps 0 to 16,000, and {{fact:tf.t128.range}} at (L8, 128), close to the null's upper end ({{fact:tf.t128.null_hi.range}}).

How I read it (reading). The coarse grouping by kind of text is learned early, before step 512, and does not sharpen later; later training reorganises the finer structure of single cells. Five checkpoints cannot place the change more precisely than "between step 0 and step 512" (not determinable here).

7. The position-$t$ view and the prefix view are not interchangeable

Claim (observed; H3 {{fact:v.H3.status}}). The point view (the 768 numbers of the residual at position $t$, the cheap measurement) and the slab view (the residuals at positions 1 to $t$, each centred over the snippets and scaled to unit length, then joined) have different leading spectral structure: $a_3$ is at most {{fact:a3.final.max}} at the final step and at most {{fact:a3.all.max}} anywhere, against a null of 0.003. H3 holds (L12 above L2 at $t = 128$), but both values are low. The slab is the more source-separated view ($\tilde W$ {{fact:Wex.slab.t128.L2_10}} at $t = 128$ in L2 to L10, against {{fact:Wex.point.t128.L2_10}}).

How I read it (reading). The raw states of layer $l$ at positions 1 to $t$ determine everything later layers compute at those positions, including their KV-cache entries. The slab view is one normalised geometry of those states (it discards each position's norm), and the point view is another. They are not interchangeable: a grouping read from single-position residuals will differ from one read from the whole prefix, and the prefix view is the more source-associated. This does not show how much information the point residual retains, only that its cosine geometry organises the snippets differently.

8. Deeper sets depend on token order; the source association survives a shuffle

Claim (observed). Shuffling tokens 1 to 127 of every snippet (keeping token 128) leaves the single-cell sets at L2 nearly unchanged (matched Jaccard {{fact:S.jacc.L2}}) and changes them a great deal at L6 to L12 ({{fact:S.jacc.L6_12}}; nulls {{fact:S.null.range}}). The raw source share $W$ stays well above chance after shuffling (at least {{fact:S.W.min_b}}): it falls at {{fact:S.W.lower_layers}} and rises at {{fact:S.W.higher_layers}} (original to shuffled: {{fact:S.W.all}}).

How I read it (reading; an association only). Deeper layers' fine structure at the last position depends on word order, as it should for a language model. The coarse association with source survives one shuffle of every snippet, consistent with finding 2: much of it is available from which tokens occur. One shuffle does not show that order plays no part, and the change in either direction by layer is not explained here.

9. One coordinate holds a large share of the variance in the first 32 tokens of the middle layers

Claim (observed). At step {{fact:final_step}}, a single coordinate carries {{fact:rogue1.final.L4_10.t4_32}} of the centred variance in L4 to L10 at positions 4 to 32, and at most {{fact:rogue1.final.t64_128.max}} at $t \ge 64$ (L2 at most {{fact:rogue1.final.L2.max}}, L12 at most {{fact:rogue1.final.L12.max}}). The share is at most {{fact:rogue1.early.max}} up to step 2,000 and {{fact:rogue1.step16000.max}} at step 16,000; the top five coordinates reach {{fact:rogue5.max}}. In L2 to L10 at $t \le 16$, each cell's first eigenfunction sits on a small group of snippets (participation number {{fact:loc.pn1.range}}, $\lambda_1$ from {{fact:loc.lam1.min}} to 1.000), apparently the same group at each of these layers (the set sizes coincide): the smallest $r = 4$ set has {{fact:loc.minset}} snippets at $t$ = 4, 8, 16. At L8, {{fact:loc.newline_frac}} of that set read a newline at $t$ = 4, 8 and 16 respectively.

Why it matters, and what is not known. The construction assumes nothing but the cosine of centred residuals. The share above is measured before each residual is scaled to unit length, so it can come from a few snippets with very large values in that coordinate; whether it also governs most snippets' directions, and so the kNN graph, was not measured. It is a potential geometry hazard, present in four of the six cells of T1's block. Whether the results survive a geometry without it is not determinable from this run: the z-scored variant (decision D6) was cut from the toy, and it is the first of the checks below.

Speculation. This looks like the "massive activations" reported in other language models, which concentrate on a few coordinates at delimiter tokens; the newline share of the localised groups points the same way. Checking it means identifying the coordinate and the snippets that carry it in each cell.

Checks I'd like to run

  1. z-scored coordinates (D6): rerun the cells with each coordinate scaled to unit variance, to see which results survive without the dominant coordinate (finding 9).
  2. A null for the clustering ratio with the real geometry: Gaussian surrogates matched to each cell's covariance through the same zoom (finding 5).
  3. A grouping that token counts cannot reveal, for example snippets of one source split by topic, or the same documents read from two different starting points, to test structure beyond token statistics (finding 2).
  4. A replicate probe: a second draw of {{fact:n_items}} snippets with the same rules. This run has one probe, so no number here has a sampling error.
  5. Held-out poetry: a corpus not in the Pile, since Gutenberg poetry may be in the training data.
  6. Denser checkpoints between 0 and 512 steps, to place the onset of the source association (finding 6).
  7. Other kernels (k = 8 or 30), since everything here uses one rule.
  8. The float32 error of C1 carried through to the sets: compare a float64 run of one checkpoint.
