The explorer is meant for a laptop or desktop screen: panels are small here, and hovering a point (to read its snippet) needs a mouse.

How to use the explorer

The grid. Rows are layers (the residual stream after block l) and a bag reference row; columns are positions t, the number of snippet tokens read. Every panel shows the same {{fact:n_items}} snippets at one cell, placed by a 2-d layout of that cell's activations. The layouts are for display only; no number on this page is computed from them. Under the grid, a strip shows one layer at t = 128 at each training checkpoint.

Pick a block. Click a panel for that cell alone, a row label for the layer's reading block (t = 4 to 128), a column header for the position's depth block (L2 to L12), or a strip button for a training block. The block's averaged walk gives the colours, which are painted on every panel so you can follow the same snippets elsewhere.

Read the side panel. Sets with their snippets and source make-up (validation only), the spectrum, the walk shares $\tilde T$ and $\tilde W$, and the retention of each function in every cell along the block's axis. Hover a point to read its snippet up to that panel's position.

  1. Pick a block. Click a panel, a row label, a column header or a strip button.
  2. Hover a point. The same snippet is ringed in every panel.
  3. Read the side panel. Sets, snippets, spectrum, retention.
