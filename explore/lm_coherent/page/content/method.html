Definitions, the choices I made, what could go wrong and how it was checked, and how to rebuild the page. The construction is the ResNet study's, applied to snippets; what is new is listed under deviations.

Model, probe and cells

Model. {{fact:model}}: 12 blocks with attention and MLP in parallel on the residual stream, width 768, rotary position embeddings on a quarter of each head (nothing positional is added to the residual), trained on the Pile for 143,000 steps of about 2M tokens. Checkpoints: steps {{fact:steps}}. The forward pass runs in float32 with eager attention.

Probe. {{fact:n_items}} snippets, {{fact:n_per_source}} per source, each from a different document and each starting at a natural boundary (a paragraph break; a line start for poetry). Maths (arXiv), code (GitHub), Wikipedia and forum posts (StackExchange) come from the Pile's test split, which the model never trained on. Poetry comes from the Gutenberg poetry corpus, which may overlap Pythia's training data (PG-19 is in the Pile). Each snippet is 128 tokens after an end-of-text token at position 0. Snippets are de-duplicated without labels: no two share the same multiset of tokens 1 to $t$ at any sampled $t$, and no two overlap by more than 0.2 in token 8-gram Jaccard. Items are shuffled, so an index says nothing about its source.

Cells. The point view of a cell is the residual stream after block $l$ at position $t$, recorded with a forward hook; $t$ is the number of snippet tokens read, and the residual at $t$ predicts token $t+1$. Positions 0 to 3 are left out: position 0 is the same for every snippet, and at 1 to 3 different snippets can have identical inputs.

Views

view$\phi(i)$ at (checkpoint, layer $l$, position $t$)cells
point$h^l_t(i)$, the residual stream at position $t$all {{fact:n_cells}}
slab$t^{-1/2}[\hat h^l_1(i), \ldots, \hat h^l_t(i)]$, every position up to $t$, each standardised as belowall {{fact:n_cells}}
bag (reference)$\frac1t \sum_{u \le t} E[x_u(i)]$, the mean input embedding of tokens 1 to $t$one per checkpoint and position

Every view goes through the same standard step: centre over the $N$ snippets, scale each row to unit length, take cosines, in float64. The slab's cosine matrix is the mean of the per-position ones, re-centred. In a causal decoder the raw states of layer $l$ at positions 1 to $t$ determine every later layer at those positions, including its KV-cache entries, so these prefix states form a chain in depth while the point residual does not; the averaged blocks used here do not need that property. The slab view is a normalised geometry of those raw states (each position's norm is discarded), not the states themselves. The bag is a function of the token counts alone, a reference for what the counts give; it is not a layer.

Glossary

item, snippet
One of the {{fact:n_items}} probe texts (a site in the theory's language).
cell
A (checkpoint, layer, position) triple; its walk $P_c$ is built from one view.
block
A run of cells along one axis, with equal weights. Reading block: one layer, positions 4 to 128. Depth block: one position, layers L2 to L12. Training block: one layer at $t = 128$, steps 0 to {{fact:final_step}} or 2,000 to {{fact:final_step}}. A single cell is also a block.
walk $P_c$, lazy walk $M_c$
$P_c$ is the Sinkhorn-scaled kernel of a cell (symmetric, rows sum to 1, zero diagonal); $M_c = \frac12(I + P_c)$.
block operator $\bar M$
The mean of the block's $M_c$. Its eigenpairs are $\lambda_k$, $\psi_k$ (with $\psi_0$ constant, $\lambda_0 = 1$).
retention $\rho_c$
$\rho_c(f) = f^\top M_c f / f^\top f$ in cell $c$; $\lambda_k$ is the mean of $\rho_c(\psi_k)$ over the block.
participation number PN
$(\sum_i \psi_i^2)^2 / \sum_i \psi_i^4$: about $N$ for a spread function, small for one sitting on a few items. Below 10 flags a localised mode.
coherent sets
The hard assignment from SEBA's $r$ sparse vectors on $\psi_0..\psi_{r-1}$; unassigned items are zero on all of them.
walk shares $T$, $W$
Equation (2) on the Overview with the grouping by the token at $t$ ($T$) or by source ($W$). Linear in $P$, so a block's value is the mean of its cells' values.
pair mixing ratio $X$
$X_c(A, B) = \frac{N-1}{|A||B|}\sum_{i \in A, j \in B} (P_c)_{ij}$: 1 for a walk that ignores sources.
span agreement $a_r$
Mean $\cos^2$ of the principal angles between the spans of $\psi_1..\psi_r$ of two cells, constant removed; null $r/(N-1)$.
matched Jaccard
Mean Jaccard index of two partitions' sets after the best one-to-one matching (Hungarian); null from 200 permutations, and a ceiling set by the set sizes.
own-source walk share
Per item and cell, the share of its row of $P_c$ that lands on its own source (validation).
conductance $\phi$, clustering ratio $\Upsilon$
See the zoom.

The zoom, precisely

For a node with snippets $S$ and the block's averaged zero-diagonal walk $\bar P^S$ (rebuilt on $S$), the conductance of $A \subset S$ is $\phi(A) = \sum_{i \in A, j \notin A} \bar P^S_{ij} / \min(|A|, n - |A|)$. Because every cell's walk has the uniform stationary measure, the block's $\phi$ is exactly the mean of the cells' $\phi$ [thm], the discrete form of the averaged boundary in Froyland's dynamic isoperimetry. Candidates are the level sets of $\psi_1$, $\psi_2$ and $\psi_3$; the cheapest with both sides at least $\max(16, \lceil 0.05 n \rceil)$ wins. With $\mu_k = 1 - \lambda_k(\bar P^S)$, the unconstrained $\psi_1$ sweep satisfies $\mu_1/2 \le h \le \phi_{\text{free}} \le \sqrt{2\mu_1}$ [thm], $h$ being the smallest conductance of any cut; the constrained $\phi^*$ satisfies only $\phi^* \ge h \ge \mu_1/2$. The clustering ratio $\Upsilon = \mu_2/\phi^*$ follows Peng, Sun and Zanetti (2015); it is at most $2\mu_2/\mu_1$.

Deviations from the ResNet study

  1. Items are snippets, and a cell has three indices (checkpoint, layer, position).
  2. One kernel rule (union {{fact:k}}-NN) and no kernel sweep.
  3. $N$ = {{fact:n_items}}.
  4. The slab and bag views are new.
  5. The walk shares $T$, $W$, the pair mixing ratio and the zoom tree are new.
  6. Eigenproblems are solved on CPU in float64, with the same algorithm.
  7. Layouts are aligned across positions (AlignedUMAP) rather than Procrustes-chained.

Diagnostics per cell (F11)

Observed. Sinkhorn converges everywhere and no cell has a localised mode below the PN flag, but the dominant coordinate is large exactly where many cells sit (L4 to L10, $t \le 32$, final step), and it is absent before step 16,000. Results that rest on those cells (single-cell sets at early positions, four of the six cells of each middle-layer reading block) should be read with it in mind.

How to reproduce

The pipeline (explore/lm_coherent/) runs on Modal: a probe unit, one GPU unit per checkpoint for the forward passes and cosine matrices, one CPU unit per checkpoint for kernels, walks, blocks, statistics and trees, and a final unit for training blocks, layouts and validation. The page is then built on the orchestration machine:

export TMPDIR=/var/tmp/cm-lm
uv run --with numpy --with matplotlib --with pillow python -I explore/lm_coherent/page/build_page.py {{fact:tag}}
bash explore/lm_coherent/page/verify.sh /var/tmp/cm-lm/shots

Construction fingerprint {{fact:fingerprint}}; transformers {{fact:transformers}}. The run (tag {{fact:tag}}) cost about {{fact:run.cost}} on Modal (an upper bound from the attempts ledger) and took {{fact:run.wall}}; run ids and the spend table are in explore/lm_coherent/README.md.
