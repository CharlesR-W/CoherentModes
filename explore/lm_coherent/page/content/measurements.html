The measurements behind the verdict, one question at a time. Captions say what is plotted; the paragraph after each figure says what I observe, and the Findings say how I read it. Every figure draws from the embedded data: hover for values.

Q0. Do the controls hold?

Two identities and three planted checks come first. C1 checks that position $t$ means "tokens 1 to $t$ read"; C2 that the bag view depends only on the token counts. P1 runs the zoom on a synthetic hierarchy with a known answer and fixes the reading threshold for $\Upsilon$ before any real tree is looked at.

Observed. Every control passes: position $t$ really means "tokens 1 to $t$ read" (C1), the bag view depends only on token counts (C2), the zoom recovers a planted hierarchy (P1) and a planted demotion (P2), and block statistics are the means of their cells' (P3). C1 is decided in float64, where a pass on tokens 1 to 64 and the full pass agree exactly (largest relative difference {{fact:c1.f64}}). The run itself is in float32, and there the two passes differ by up to {{fact:c1.f32.max}} ({{fact:c1.f32.by_layer}}); that is reported as a hazard rather than a failure (decision D12). The bag view is bitwise identical for the original and reordered probes. The zoom recovers the planted tree exactly, with planted splits at $\Upsilon \ge$ {{fact:p1.min_planted_upsilon}} and structureless ones at most {{fact:p1.max_structureless_upsilon}}, so the reading threshold stays at {{fact:threshold}}. The planted demotion check gives {{fact:p2.summary}}; block statistics equal the means of their cells' to {{fact:p3.maxdev}} on the real data.

Which blocks find sets that line up with sources?

The four questions below are about walk shares and agreement. This one is about the coherent sets themselves: compare each block's $r = 4$ sets with the five sources (validation only).

Observed. Reading blocks reproduce the source partition closely ({{fact:rd.ratio.L4_12}} of the best score their set sizes allow, at L4 to L12). Single cells, depth blocks and training blocks are associated with source above chance (each beats its own permutation null) but do not reproduce it: single cells reach {{fact:cell.ratio.point.range}} of their caps. At step 0 no reading block exceeds {{fact:rd.sj.step0.max}}; by step 512 they are at {{fact:rd.sj.step512.range}}. The slab cells at $t \ge 64$, whose geometry is itself an average over positions, match sources about as well as the reading blocks. See finding 1.

Q1. Is the walk tied to the token just read, or to the source?

In plain words: $\tilde T$ is how much more often than chance a walk step joins two snippets that read the same token at position $t$; $\tilde W$ is the same for two snippets from the same source. Both are 0 for a walk that ignores the grouping and 1 for one that never leaves it ("excess" means measured above chance). Training enters as five panels side by side, one per checkpoint, on one colour scale.

Observed. At step {{fact:final_step}}, $\tilde T$ is {{fact:Tex.L2.range}} at L2 at every position and {{fact:Tex.L12.range}} at L12, so E1 is met. It is already {{fact:Tex.L2.step0.range}} at L2 at step 0: the untrained network's early residual is tied to the token it reads. $\tilde W$ is {{fact:Wex.step0.range}} in every cell at step 0. At the final step, in L6 to L12, it rises with $t$ until $t$ = 32 or 64 and then flattens or falls back (L8: {{fact:Wex.L8.t4}} at $t$ = 4, {{fact:Wex.L8.t32}} at 32, {{fact:Wex.L8.t128}} at 128). From $t = 4$ to 128 it gains {{fact:H1.diffs}} at L6 to L12, short of the 0.2 that H1 predicted: H1 is not met. The bag row rises from {{fact:Wex.bag.t4}} to {{fact:Wex.bag.t128}}, above every layer at every $t$ (the largest layer value is {{fact:Wex.max.point}}). Targets: E1 ({{fact:v.E1.status}}), H1 ({{fact:v.H1.status}}).

Q2. When does the walk stop linking maths and poetry?

The pair mixing ratio $X(A, B)$ is the walk mass between sources $A$ and $B$, scaled so that a walk ignoring sources gives 1. Below 1, the walk avoids crossing between them. Wikipedia against forum posts is the prose-to-prose contrast.

To see what this means for single snippets, follow two of them. The ten follow items are the first two of each source in probe order, which is random, so the choice does not depend on any result.

Observed. $X(\text{maths}, \text{poetry})$ drops below 0.5 at $t = 8$ in each of L6 to L12 (first $t$ below 0.5: {{fact:X.mp.first_t_below_half.L6_12}}) and is {{fact:X.mp.L6_12.t32_128}} for $t \ge 32$. At (L8, 16) it is {{fact:X.mp.L8.t16}} against a permutation band of {{fact:X.mp.L8.t16.band}}, so H2 is met; the same cell gave {{fact:X.mp.L8.step0}} at step 0. At (L8, 16) maths and poetry are the {{fact:X.mp.L8.t16.rank}}rd most separated of the {{fact:X.pairs.n}} pairs: code against poetry is {{fact:X.cp.L8.t16}}, wiki against forum {{fact:X.wf.L8.t16}}, and code against forum {{fact:X.cf.L8.t16}}. L2 keeps mixing maths and poetry ({{fact:X.mp.L2.range}}); the bag reference separates them almost completely by $t = 128$ ({{fact:X.mp.bag.t128}}). The two default follow snippets (items {{fact:follow.maths.item}} and {{fact:follow.poetry.item}}) send {{fact:follow.maths.own.L8}} and {{fact:follow.poetry.own.L8}} of their L8 walk to their own source across positions, and {{fact:follow.maths.own.L12.t128}} and {{fact:follow.poetry.own.L12.t128}} at (L12, 128). Even so they share a single-cell $r = 4$ set in {{fact:follow.same_cells}} cells at the final step: the cells' own sets are not sorted by source. Target: H2 ({{fact:v.H2.status}}).

Q3. Does position $t$ tell the same story as the prefix?

Two views of the same cell are compared. The point view is the 768 numbers of the residual at position $t$: the cheap measurement. The slab view takes the residuals at positions 1 to $t$, centres each position over the snippets, scales it to unit length and joins them: a normalised view of the layer's states up to $t$. Those raw states determine everything later layers compute at these positions, including their entries in the KV cache; the slab view keeps their directions, not their norms. Agreement between the two views' eigenfunctions asks whether the cheap view organises the snippets the same way; it is not a measure of information.

Observed. Agreement is low everywhere: $a_3$ is at most {{fact:a3.final.max}} at the final step ({{fact:a3.t128.final}} at $t = 128$) and at most {{fact:a3.all.max}} at any checkpoint, and the two views' $r = 4$ sets match at {{fact:ps.jacc.final.range}}. H3 is met (L12 above L2 at $t = 128$), but both values are small. The slab separates sources far more: at $t = 128$ its $\tilde W$ is {{fact:Wex.slab.t128.L2_10}} at L2 to L10 against {{fact:Wex.point.t128.L2_10}} for the point view, and its sets match sources at {{fact:cell.sj.slab.t64_128.L2_10}} for $t \ge 64$ (L2 to L10). So the two views are not interchangeable, and the prefix view is the more source-associated; this does not say how much information the point residual retains. Target: H3 ({{fact:v.H3.status}}).

Q4. When in training does source separation appear?

Observed. At (L8, 128), $\tilde W$ is {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step0}} at step 0 and {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step512}} by step 512, then {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step2000}}, {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step16000}} and {{fact:Wex.L8.t128.step143000}}: the association with source appears within the first 512 steps and then drifts down a little. H4 is met. The L8 reading block's match to sources follows the same course ({{fact:rd.sj.L8.by_step}} over the five checkpoints). Single-cell sets settle much less: against the final checkpoint's sets they score {{fact:tf.t16}} at (L8, 16) for steps 0 to 16,000, and only {{fact:tf.t128.range}} at (L8, 128), barely above the null's upper end ({{fact:tf.t128.null_hi.range}}). Target: H4 ({{fact:v.H4.status}}).

Q5. Does the order of the earlier tokens matter?

For each snippet, the reordered probe S shuffles tokens 1 to 127 and keeps token 128, and is read at $t = 128$ only. The two probes then have the same snippets, the same token counts and the same last token, so comparing them changes only the order of the earlier tokens. The bag view cannot tell them apart (C2). This is an association, not a causal claim about what the model uses.

Observed. Reordering changes the sets little at L2 (matched Jaccard {{fact:S.jacc.L2}}), more at L4 ({{fact:S.jacc.L4}}) and a great deal at L6 to L12 ({{fact:S.jacc.L6_12}}; nulls {{fact:S.null.range}}). The walk's association with source survives the shuffle but moves: raw $W$ falls at {{fact:S.W.lower_layers}} and rises at {{fact:S.W.higher_layers}} (original to reordered: {{fact:S.W.all}}). This is an association, not a statement about what the model uses.
