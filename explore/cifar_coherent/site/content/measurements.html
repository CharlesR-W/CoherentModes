Interactive alignment views

The four figures below are drawn in the browser from the spectral-alignment exports, with their own controls (depth pair, checkpoint, distance, kernel rule). Every cross-depth summary they print uses the different-seed pairings; same-seed pairings are labelled "shared initial draws". Definitions: operator distances, DTW, nulls, seed floors.

Which modes, and which images. For {{fact:al.vec_cells}} snapshots (every network at step {{fact:al.final_step}} and ResNet-20 seed 0 at every checkpoint; kernel rule {{fact:default_mode}}) the leading $64$ eigenfunctions are shipped as $16$-bit values ($d_k$ recomputed from them differs from the full-precision value by at most {{fact:al.int16_dk_max_error}}), and the figure below computes in the browser the mode-overlap matrix $G = U_{64}(A)^\top U_{64}(B)$, shown as $G^2$ (the sign of an eigenfunction is arbitrary); the cumulative block overlap $\|G_{[:i,:j]}\|_F^2 / \min(i, j)$ (on the diagonal, $1 - d_i$); the principal angles between the two leading-$k$ spans, as $\cos^2$; and the per-image discrepancy $\delta_i = [(P_k(A) - P_k(B))^2]_{ii}$, which sums to $2k\,d_k$. Entries of $G$ for single modes are meaningful only at isolated, reproducible eigenvalues: inside a group of near-equal eigenvalues the eigenfunctions can rotate among themselves, so $G$ can look mixed while the spans agree; the cumulative overlap and the principal angles are the quantities to compare there.
