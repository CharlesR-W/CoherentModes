Appendices

The main text (Overview, Primer, Explorer, Experiments, Method) is meant to be read on its own. The appendices hold the construction choices, the checks that the method is sound, the full definitions, the charts the experiments do not show, and how to reproduce the site.

  1. Construction choices: the kernel rules and the sweep behind them, the cosine versus dot-product kernel, the average versus the product operator, construction ablations, the choice of $r$ and of the cut $k$, layouts, and variants of the construction.
  2. Is the method sound? Baselines, nulls and stability: the same numbers from simpler methods and from null data, agreement between sources, forced extraction, stability, planted benchmarks and the robustness of the distances.
  3. Definitions and statistics: defaults, every comparison statistic, null and ceiling, and the definitions behind each experiment.
  4. Reference charts: training curves, class decodability, class make-up tables, and every chart the experiments moved out of the main text, grouped by experiment.
  5. Reproduce: data, commands and the release build.
