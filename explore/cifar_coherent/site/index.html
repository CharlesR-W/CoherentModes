Appendices

The main text (Overview, Primer, Explorer, Findings, Method) is meant to be read on its own. The appendices hold the systematic charts, the comparisons with simpler methods and nulls, and the full definitions behind them, for a reader checking a claim or looking for a chart the main text does not show.

  1. Measurements: every comparison statistic as charts over sites and training (agreement with the final checkpoint and between sites, class make-up, vehicles against animals, image statistics, gradients, decodability). The figures in Findings are drawn from here.
  2. Baselines and nulls: the same numbers from simpler methods (PCA, k-means, spectral clustering, ...) and from null data, construction ablations, planted benchmarks and the transfer-operator pilot.
  3. Method details: the kernel sweep, average versus product, exact definitions of every statistic, baseline and null, and how to reproduce the site.
    4. Alignment: distances between snapshots of one network and across networks, by depth and over training.
