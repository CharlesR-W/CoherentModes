1. The whole state separates sources more than any residual layer, and earlier

Claim (observed). At $t = 128$ the whole-state cell (stack, L24) has $\tilde W$ = 0.74; the best residual layer (L20) has 0.53. The gap is largest early: at $t = 4$ the whole state is at 0.49, residual layers at 0.22 to 0.40, and the bag of tokens at 0.31. Stacks from L8 upwards are all within 0.02 of the whole state at $t = 128$ (0.73 to 0.75), so the layers above L8 add little source separation to what the lower layers' states already hold.

How I read it (reading). In early reading, the state carries more about which kind of text this is than the token counts or any one residual do. By $t = 128$ the bag reaches 0.68, and reordering tokens 1 to 127 leaves the whole state's sets nearly unchanged (matched Jaccard 0.84 between the original and reordered probes at L24, 0.87 to 0.91 at L8 to L20). So at full length most of what the state separates on this probe is vocabulary, as in the Pythia study (its finding 2). The probe has no grouping that token counts fail to reveal, so it cannot show more than that.

2. The residual stream is a poor stand-in for the state

Claim (observed). Span agreement $a_3$ between the residual at layer $l$ and the stack of states up to $l$ is 0.06 to 0.44 over all layers and positions (null 0.003). Against the layer's own state, $a_3$ is 0.04 to 0.49, except at L24, where it reaches 0.86 at $t = 4$. The layer state and the stack mostly agree (0.4 to 0.96), least at L16 (0.37 to 0.43) and L24 (0.26 to 0.7).

How I read it (reading). Coherent sets read off the residual stream, the usual object of interpretability, describe a different partition from the sets of what the model actually carries forward. That echoes Pythia's finding 7, that the position-$t$ view and the prefix slab are not interchangeable. Here the comparison is with the exact carried state rather than a proxy for it.

3. The layer states are uneven: L16's state separates sources much less

Claim (observed). Layer-state $\tilde W$ at $t = 128$ is 0.77 to 0.78 at L4, L12 and L20, but 0.40 at L16 (and 0.60 at L8 and 0.59 at L24). The same layers have the lowest state-vs-stack agreement.

What else it could be (speculation). Some layers' states may be organised around something other than source, such as position or formatting. The view's equal weighting of conv buffer and SSM state (decision D-M1) may also matter. The state_parts diagnostic (Method) separates the two parts; I have not read it further.

4. Early residuals follow the token just read; source separation is mostly in place by $t = 16$

Claim (observed). $\tilde T$ falls with depth at every position, from 0.16 to 0.18 at L4 to 0.04 to 0.06 at L24 (H1 met). Between $t = 4$ and 128, $\tilde W$ grows by only 0.00 to 0.17 at L12 to L24, so H2 (at least 0.2) is not met. Most of the growth happens by $t = 16$, after which the residual's source separation is flat or falls slightly. $X(\text{maths}, \text{poetry})$ at (L16, 16) is 0.29 (H3 met). At initialisation, $\tilde W$ at (L16, 128) is 0.0001, against 0.44 trained (H5 met).

5. Mamba and Pythia find largely the same structure in the same snippets

Claim (observed). The two studies' headline zoom cuts (the root of T1: L8 in Pythia, L16 in Mamba) put 927 of 1,000 snippets on the same side (matched Jaccard 0.86; null about 0.35). At $t = 128$, point-vs-point $a_3$ between matched depths is 0.70 to 0.75 in the lower third and 0.33 to 0.55 above (H7 met). Pythia's prefix slab against Mamba's stacked state reaches 0.86 to 0.89 from Pythia L4 / Mamba L8 to Pythia L10 / Mamba L20, and drops at the top layers (0.33).

How I read it (reading). At this scale and on this probe, the coherent-set picture mostly reflects the snippets and the shared training data (the Pile, the same tokenizer), not the architecture. The views that summarise the whole prefix (Pythia's slab, Mamba's state) agree more than the single-position residuals do.

6. The zoom on the whole state splits code, maths and forum from wiki and poetry

Claim (observed). The root cut of T4, the reading block of the whole state, puts 594 of the 600 maths, code and forum snippets on one side and 393 of the 400 wiki and poetry snippets on the other. Its clustering ratio is 1.99, below the threshold of 3, so it is drawn as a weak split. T1's root cut is the same partition, less cleanly (code 1 / 199, poetry 200 / 0: H6 met). No root split in the four trees reaches the threshold.
