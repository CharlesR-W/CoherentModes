The explorer is meant for a laptop or desktop screen: panels are small here, and hovering a point (to read its snippet) needs a mouse.

How to use the explorer

The grid. Rows are layers and a bag reference row; columns are positions t, the number of snippet tokens read. The View switch chooses what a layer row measures: the point view (the residual stream after block l at t), the state view (layer l's own recurrent state after t tokens) or the stack view (the states of layers 1 to l; at L24 that is everything the model carries forward). Every panel shows the same {{fact:n_items}} snippets at one cell, placed by a 2-d layout of that cell in that view. The layouts are for display only; no number on this page is computed from them. Under the grid, a strip shows one layer at t = 128 at the HF initialisation and trained.

Pick a block. Click a panel for that cell alone, a row label for the layer's reading block (t = 4 to 128, in the current view), a column header for the position's depth block (L4 to L24, point view), or a strip panel for that cell at either checkpoint. The block's averaged walk gives the colours, which are painted on every panel so you can follow the same snippets elsewhere.

Read the side panel. Sets with their snippets and source make-up (validation only), the spectrum, the walk shares $\tilde T$ and $\tilde W$, and the retention of each function in every cell along the block's axis. Hover a point to read its snippet up to that panel's position.

  1. Pick a view and a block. point, state or stack; then a panel, a row label or a column header.
  2. Hover a point. The same snippet is ringed in every panel.
  3. Read the side panel. Sets, snippets, spectrum, retention.
