Matched Jaccard

For two hard labelings $A$ ($r_A$ sets) and $B$ ($r_B$ sets), with unassigned images in no set: $J_{ij} = |A_i \cap B_j| / |A_i \cup B_j|$; a Hungarian matching of $\min(r_A, r_B)$ pairs maximises the total $J$; the score is the mean matched $J$, in $[0, 1]$. Null: the label vector of $A$, unassigned entries included, is permuted uniformly (set sizes and unassigned count kept); {{fact:q.n_perm}} permutations from one seeded bank; bands are the 2.5 to 97.5 percentiles, $\mu_0$ is the null mean. Size ceiling (against a fixed reference labeling): the largest score any sets of $A$'s sizes could reach. Normalised excess $= (\text{score} - \mu_0) / (\text{ceiling} - \mu_0)$.

The $r = 2$ sets against the animal / non-animal partition

Animal = the class groups dogs, other mammals, birds, reptiles and invertebrates ({{fact:q.va.n_animal}} images); non-animal = the other five groups ({{fact:q.va.n_other}} images). This fixed 2-set reference labeling is scored against each single-cell block's two $r = 2$ SEBA sets by matched Jaccard (for $2 \times 2$, the better of the two pairings), with null, size ceiling and normalised excess as in the callout.

Image statistics against eigenfunctions and SEBA vectors

Twelve statistics of each probe image's 64 × 64 thumbnail (the centre crop of the model input), with pixel values in $[0, 1]$ and $Y = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B$: mean R, G, B and Y; RMS contrast (standard deviation of $Y$); mean HSV saturation; border minus centre means of R, G, B and Y (border = the 1,792 pixels within 8 px of the edge, centre = the central 32 × 32); the norm of the three colour differences; and the share of the DFT energy of $Y - \bar Y$ at radius above 16 cycles per image. These are the 32 px definitions of the ResNet study with every length doubled. They are also colourings in the explorer (Colour by → Image statistics).
