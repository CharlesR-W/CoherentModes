Every definition and setting, in the order the pipeline runs. The construction is the one in the ResNet-20 study, run unchanged (same code, same five kernel rules, same SEBA); only the model, the probe images and the sites differ.

Glossary

  • Probe image. One of the $N = {{fact:n_images}}$ ImageNet-100 images fed to the network; the same images, indexed $i = 1..N$, are used at every site.
  • Site. A place in the network where activations are recorded: $L1$ to $L12$, the CLS token after transformer block $l$, read out through the model's final LayerNorm ({{fact:n_sites}} in all).
  • Cell. One site (there is one checkpoint: the released weights). Its data are the $N$ activation vectors of the probe images at that site.
  • Block. A run of consecutive sites $a..b$ whose operators are averaged (a layer block; 78 in all). A single-cell block ($a = b$) is ordinary spectral clustering (diffusion maps) of that site alone.
  • Kernel rule. The recipe that turns a cell's cosine distances into a symmetric, non-negative weight matrix $K$ with zero diagonal. There are five (table); the default is the {{fact:default_mode}}.
  • Walk $P_c$. The Sinkhorn scaling $\mathrm{diag}(v)\,K\,\mathrm{diag}(v)$ of cell $c$'s kernel: symmetric, non-negative, zero on the diagonal, every row and column summing to 1.
  • Lazy walk $M_c$. $M_c = \tfrac12(I + P_c)$. Same eigenvectors as $P_c$, eigenvalues in $[0, 1]$.
  • Block operator $\bar M$. The equal-weight average $\bar M = \frac{1}{|B|} \sum_{c \in B} M_c$ over the sites of block $B$.
  • Eigenfunction $\psi_k$. The $k$-th unit eigenvector of $\bar M$ in decreasing order of eigenvalue, read as a function on the probe images. $\psi_0 = \mathbf 1/\sqrt N$ is the constant.
  • Spectrum. The eigenvalues $1 = \lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots$ of $\bar M$; for a unit eigenfunction, $1 - \lambda_k$ is its average lazy penalty over the block's sites. For SEBA with $r$ sets the relevant gap is $\lambda_{r-1} - \lambda_r$.
  • Retention $\rho_c$. For a function $f$ and any site $c$, inside or outside the block, $\rho_c(f) = f^\top M_c f / f^\top f \in [0, 1]$. A block eigenvalue is the mean retention of its eigenfunction over the block: $\lambda_k = \frac{1}{|B|} \sum_{c \in B} \rho_c(\psi_k)$.
  • SEBA vector. One of the $r$ sparse vectors that SEBA returns from $\mathrm{span}(\psi_0, \dots, \psi_{r-1})$: soft-thresholded, sign-fixed, scaled to a maximum of 1, negative entries clipped to 0.
  • Coherent set. The images at which SEBA vector $j$ is the largest of the $r$ and positive (the hard assignment), numbered by reliability.
  • Unassigned. An image at which all $r$ SEBA vectors are 0 after clipping; drawn grey.
  • Participation number (PN). $(\sum_i \psi_i^2)^2 / \sum_i \psi_i^4$, roughly how many images a mode is spread over.
  • Matched Jaccard. For two collections of hard sets: the Jaccard overlap of every pair, a one-to-one matching that maximises the total, and the mean over matched pairs; null band from permuting one side's labels.

Model, probe, sites

  • Model. DINOv2 ViT-S/14 (dinov2_vits14, 12 blocks, width 384, 6 heads), the released weights (Hugging Face facebook/dinov2-small, key-mapped into the official hub architecture), frozen, 224 px inputs (resize 256, centre crop 224). Activations come from the float64 re-implementation of each block used by the earlier ViT run (explore/vit_coherent/acts.py; maximum deviation from the hub model's float32 readout $1.3 \times 10^{-4}$).
  • Probe. $N = {{fact:n_images}}$ images of clane9/imagenet-100 (100 ImageNet classes; images stored at 160 px on the short side, so upsampled before cropping): 20 per class, drawn with numpy.RandomState(0) from that class's 50 ImageNet-val images in the earlier run's probe set. Drawing a class-balanced probe is the only use of labels before the sets exist.
  • Sites ($S = {{fact:n_sites}}$): $v_l(x) = \mathrm{LN}(h_l(x))_\text{CLS}$, $l = 1..12$, with $h_l$ the residual stream after block $l$ and LN the model's final LayerNorm (its own readout). $L0$, the CLS token before any block, is the same vector for every image and is left out.
  • Class groups (colour and validation only). The 100 classes are shown in 10 groups merged from the 18 WordNet superclasses of classes.json (themselves a fixed rule, superclasses.py): dogs; other mammals; birds; reptiles; invertebrates; food and plants (with fungi); clothing (with other coverings); devices (with instruments and household commodities); containers & vehicles; structures (with furniture). The tooltip shows the fine class. WordNet quirks are kept (an ambulance is a container).

Per-site operator

For site $c$ with activations $h_i \in \mathbb{R}^{384}$:

$$ \hat h_i = \frac{h_i - \bar h}{\lVert h_i - \bar h\rVert}, \quad \bar h = \tfrac1N \textstyle\sum_i h_i, \qquad C_{ij} = \langle \hat h_i, \hat h_j \rangle, \qquad d_{ij} = 1 - C_{ij}. $$

$K$ is a symmetric non-negative kernel with $K_{ii} = 0$ from one of five kernel rules. Sinkhorn: find $u$ with $\sum_j \exp(\log K_{ij} + u_i + u_j) = 1$ for all $i$, in the log domain in float64 (ten fixed-point steps, then line-searched Newton); every site and rule here reached a maximum row error below $2 \times 10^{-12}$ (largest $1.3 \times 10^{-12}$). Then

$$ P = \mathrm{diag}(v)\, K\, \mathrm{diag}(v), \qquad M = \tfrac12 (I + P), \qquad f^\top (I - P)\, f = \tfrac12 \sum_{i,j} P_{ij}\,(f_i - f_j)^2 . $$

The zero diagonal and the lazy walk are a stated variant of the project's theory (THEORY.md, Section 3.1); the reasons are given in the ResNet study's Method tab (self-weight under sharp kernels, a common laziness, no slow modes from near-bipartite alternation).

The five kernel rules

$d_{ij} = 1 - C_{ij}$, $k = 15$, $K_{ii} = 0$ throughout. $d_k$ = median over images of the distance to the $k$-th nearest other image.

Blocks and retention

  • Layer blocks: sites $a..b$, all $1 \le a \le b \le 12$ (78). Block operator $\bar M = \frac{1}{|B|} \sum_{c \in B} M_c$; its leading eigenfunctions are the unit functions orthogonal to the constant and to each other with the smallest average penalty over the block. No map between layers is used.
  • Eigenpairs by a dense float64 symmetric eigensolver; $\psi_1..\psi_8$ stored with unit norm, sign fixed so the largest-magnitude entry is positive, quantised to int16 relative to $\max_i |\psi_k(i)|$ (int8 in the browser).
  • Retention $\rho_c(f)$ is stored for $\psi_1..\psi_8$ and every SEBA vector at every site, inside and outside the block. A high $\lambda_k$ is a high average, not high retention at every site; the retention strip shows each site's value.
  • The averaging over sites follows Froyland's dynamic Laplacian (Froyland 2015; Froyland and Junge 2018) and Banisch and Koltai's space-time diffusion maps (2017), with depth in place of time.

SEBA sets

Sparse Eigenbasis Approximation (Froyland, Rock and Sakellariou 2019, Algorithm 3.1), ported from the authors' reference code. Input $V = [\psi_0, \dots, \psi_{r-1}]$ (constant included), $r \in \{2, 3, 4, 6, 8\}$, $\mu = 0.99/\sqrt N$; iterate soft-thresholding and the polar rotation until $\lVert R_\text{new} - R \rVert \le 10^{-14}$. Flip each column to a positive sum, divide by its maximum, order by reliability (decreasing minimum entry), clip negatives to 0. Hard label: $\arg\max_j S_{ij}$ if positive, else unassigned. The rotation and permutation are stored, so the browser reconstructs the full vectors for loadings and retention.

Layouts (display only)

  • umap.AlignedUMAP across the 12 sites (identity relations between consecutive sites), n_neighbors=15, min_dist=0.1, alignment_regularisation=0.02, random_state=0, on the top 50 principal components of each site's centred cosine Gram matrix $C$.
  • No quantity is computed from the 2-d coordinates.

Head loadings

The ResNet explorer's "channel loadings" use each cell's spatially averaged channels. Here the per-site "channels" are the 6 attention heads of block $l$: $\lVert A_{l,h}(x) \rVert_2$, the norm of head $h$'s output written to the CLS position (after the output projection and LayerScale). For the selected $\psi_k$ or SEBA vector $f$: Pearson $\mathrm{corr}(f, \lVert A_{l,h} \rVert)$ per head, and $R^2$ of in-sample least squares of centred $f$ on the six head norms at every site (descriptive, not held out; chance about $6/N$). There is no classifier head, so the ResNet panel's "alignment with the classifier" is absent.

Relation to the earlier ViT run

An earlier page on this model (explore/vit_coherent/viewer, "Smoothness across depth") followed a different plan: 10,000 images, per-layer Sinkhorn walks with self-loops kept, a von Mises-Fisher kernel ($\kappa = 5$, with a self-tuned $k = 7$ kernel as second default) chosen by a label-free stability rule, smoothness profiles $s_l^t(f) = f^\top P_l^{2t} f$ and $\Delta$-modes between pairs of layers, SEBA only for single layers ($r \ge 8$). This page instead runs the ResNet construction (block averages of lazy walks over runs of layers, SEBA at $r = 2..8$) on a 2,000-image subset of the same probe, so that the two networks can be read with the same plots. Results of the two constructions are not interchangeable.
