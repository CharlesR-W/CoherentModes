Layout

The kernel rule decides which probe images count as neighbours in each cell before the walk $P_c$ is built. With cosine similarity $C$ and distance $d = 1 - C$, a temperature rule uses $K_{ij} = e^{-\kappa d_{ij}}$; $\kappa$ is the inverse temperature. The answer depends on the rule, so it is exposed here rather than hidden.

  • kNN graph, k = 15 (default): link $i$ and $j$ if either is among the other's 15 nearest neighbours. Rank-based, no $\kappa$; the same neighbourhood size as the UMAP layouts.
  • per-point perplexity 100 / 300: each image gets its own $\kappa_i$ so that its neighbour distribution has perplexity 100 (or 300, coarser); the t-SNE affinity. Expect results close to the kNN graph.
  • kNN scale (flat reference): one $\kappa = 1/d_{15}$ per cell, 1.5 to 3.6 across $L1$ to $L12$ here. A nearly flat kernel (15-NN share of kernel mass 2% to 5%).
  • half-mass, calibrated: one $\kappa$ per cell chosen so the 15 nearest neighbours carry half the kernel mass; $\kappa$ 10 to 16 here. In the ResNet's middle layers this rule put leading modes on near-duplicate pairs of images; the PN row under the spectrum shows whether that happens here.

The last two rules use one temperature per cell; in the ResNet they failed in opposite ways (too flat, too sharp) and are kept here for the same comparison. The side panel gives $\kappa$ and the median 15-NN kernel mass over the block's cells; values for every site are in Method.

Tour
How to use the explorer

The grid. One row (the released DINOv2 ViT-S/14 weights; there are no training checkpoints), twelve columns: the CLS token after blocks 1 to 12, read out through the model's final LayerNorm ($L1$ to $L12$). Each cell shows the same 2,000 probe images, placed by a 2-d UMAP of that cell's activations (AlignedUMAP across sites). Positions are for display only; no number on this page is computed from them.

The block (outlined) is a run of consecutive sites (a layer block); one cell alone is a single-cell block. Its block operator $\bar M$ averages the lazy walks $M_c$ of its cells. The colouring comes from that block only and is painted on every cell, so you can follow the same images to the other layers.

The side panel describes the selection: the spectrum of $\bar M$, representative images, retention $\rho_c$ in every cell of the row, the cell under the pointer enlarged, channel loadings, and per-cell numbers.

Extras. The class groups (10 groups of the 100 ImageNet classes; fine class in the tooltip), the attention-head norms used as "channels", and the pixel statistics only colour and validate; none enters the kernel or the sets. Construction and conventions are those of the ResNet-20 explorer, unchanged. Definitions: Method.
